The jobsite mindset

Do the thinking before the floor is permanent

  • Confirm the room goals, budget, subfloor, humidity, sound requirements, and finish expectations before selecting wood.
  • Document each stage so the homeowner, designer, or contractor understands what happens next.
  • Use the least invasive repair or maintenance method that still produces a durable, beautiful floor.
