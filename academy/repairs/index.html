Lesson 8

Repairs and restoration

Even durable floors experience wear, water, layout changes, and aging. Repair work should preserve as much original beauty as possible.

Repair vs restoration

Repairs

Use repairs when the general floor is good but small areas need attention: water damage, broken boards, loosened boards, missing pieces after wall redesign, or poor previous repairs.

Restoration

Use restoration when vintage flooring, patterns, inlays, or medallions are damaged and missing pieces need custom cutting and blending.

Diagnosis sequence

  1. Find the source of the damage before replacing boards.
  2. Determine whether the repair is structural, cosmetic, or both.
  3. Match species, width, thickness, grade, direction, and age as closely as possible.
  4. Blend sanding and finish so the repair does not read as a patch.

Professional standard

Good repair work should make the floor look intentional again. The goal is not just to fill a hole; it is to bring the floor back into the room's design.
