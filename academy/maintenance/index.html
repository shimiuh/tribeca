Lesson 9

Maintenance

A maintenance coat can extend floor life and avoid a full resand when the top finish layer is wearing out.

Buffing and recoating

Most polyurethane floors have three coats. Do not wait until all coats are destroyed. A buff and coat refreshes the top layer so the floor looks clean again without full sanding.

For oil- and water-based polyurethane, the top layer is lightly sanded in a screening process, then a new coat is applied. Because it is clear, large furniture may not always need to be moved far from the work area.

Natural oil maintenance

Natural oil wax penetrates wood instead of sitting on top like polyurethane. It looks more natural and can be spot maintained, but it requires more frequent care.
