The sequence

  1. Consult: define goals, constraints, finish expectations, budget, and schedule.
  2. Inspect: measure, evaluate existing floor, identify subfloor and moisture concerns.
  3. Select: choose prefinished or unfinished, solid or engineered, finish system, and installation method.
  4. Prepare: remove carpet, staples, tack strips, debris, and anything that hides the real floor condition.
  5. Foundation: level the subfloor and install sound or moisture control when needed.
  6. Install: use nail-down, glue-down, combination, or floating methods based on material and structure.
  7. Finish: sand, stain, seal, and protect when using unfinished or refinished flooring.
  8. Repair: correct damaged boards, missing wood, or vintage patterns with matching and blending.
  9. Maintain: screen, buff, recoat, oil, or spot repair before full refinishing becomes necessary.

Final standard

A successful wood floor is not just installed. It is planned, supported, controlled for movement, finished intentionally, and maintained before damage gets expensive.
