Carpet removal

If floors are covered with carpeting, remove it before installing new wood flooring. Cut and lift carpeting, remove padding, tack strips, staples, nails, and debris. Only after removal can the floor beneath be evaluated accurately.

Environmentally sensitive disposal and carpet recycling are preferable where available. Pricing should remain provisional until the hidden floor condition is known.

Tools and protection

  • Sharp retractable knife, duct tape, hammer, pry bar, pliers, knee pads, broom, and vacuum.
  • Dust mask and sturdy gloves to protect against dirt, staples, tacks, and protruding nails.
  • Trash bags, contractor bags, or disposal bins for carpet, underlay, tack strip, and staples.

Removal sequence

  1. Cut carpet into manageable strips, often about four feet wide.
  2. Pull strips away from one wall, stack them, roll them, and remove them from the room.
  3. Repeat for the underlay or cushion beneath the carpet.
  4. Remove tack strips around the walls with a pry bar or hammer and flathead screwdriver.
  5. Pull staples and small nails, then sweep, vacuum, and do a final hand inspection.

Cost-control option

Because skilled flooring crews are expensive, a client can sometimes reduce cost by removing carpet before the professional floor work begins.
