Creativity and ideas

There are no limits to your creativity and ideas, but there some limits in your job. You need to design a floor your clients will love, stay within their budget, and work with the contractor’s job conditions. To meet your goals, you need the best information and expertise on wood products, installation methods, and stains and finishes.

Experience

We can lend you our expertise, knowledge, and experience in all aspects of wood flooring.  We have years of experience of working with designers, and we can give you all the resources you need so you can design the most durable and beautiful floor for your clients.

we will stay in touch

Throughout the duration of the project, we will stay in touch with you, sending you daily updates and pictures of the job site, so you can confidently leave flooring with the experts and focus on what you do best.

Who are we?
Since 2007, Tribeca Flooring, a revolutionary non-union wood flooring company, has been at the forefront of hardwood flooring in the Metro New York area. Over the years, we’ve noticed how daunting and confusing it can be for our clients to sift through all the information available about every detail of flooring and installation in order to choose what works best for them.
